About Me

I am a Research Data Analyst at Mississippi State University, where I work on genomics data pipelines in Dr. Li's lab. I hold an M.S. in Computer Science (4.0 GPA) with a focus on healthcare data analytics, dimensional modeling, and BI reporting.

Most of what I do comes down to one thing: making data trustworthy. I re-architected a pipeline from an HPC cluster to standard workstations, cut a step's memory footprint from 32 GB to under 1 GB, and root-caused an intermittent failure that had been quietly discarding an hour of compute per run — it turned out to be host/guest clock drift, not the cause that had been reported.

My portfolio projects follow the same principle. Each one ships with a validation suite, because a metric computed over a fact table that silently duplicated rows in a join is still a number, still plausible, and completely wrong. Every project runs end to end from a clean clone.
